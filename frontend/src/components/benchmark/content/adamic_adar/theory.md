# Theory

Adamic-Adar improves upon Common Neighbors by giving more weight to common neighbors with lower degree. Sharing a rare neighbor is more meaningful than sharing a highly connected one.

## Key Concepts

- **Weighted approach**: Rare neighbors get higher weights
- **Logarithmic penalty**: Penalizes high-degree neighbors logarithmically
- **Discriminative**: More accurate than simple common neighbors
- **Social insight**: Unique connections are more valuable
- **No parameters**: Works without any tuning
